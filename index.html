Introduction

About Me

I’m a B.Tech student in Computer Science and Engineering at Shri Ramswaroop Memorial University in Lucknow, with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. I began the programme in 2025 and I’m building my foundation across programming, mathematics and core computer science.

What draws me to AI and Machine Learning is the idea of teaching computers to find patterns and learn from data. I’m still at the beginning of that journey, and I enjoy working through new concepts one step at a time.

I’m also interested in frontend development — turning an idea into a page that people can actually open and use. This portfolio and my academic projects are where I practise HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Java and Python are the languages I’m learning with, and I use data structures, algorithms and problem solving to train myself to think more clearly about code. My goal is to grow into a skilled software engineer who writes clean, reliable code and keeps learning as technology changes.
